# RetailIQ — Business Intelligence Findings & Executive Recommendations

## Executive Summary
This document synthesizes key business intelligence insights derived from 1,800 transactions across 10 Indian metropolitan markets throughout 2024.

---

## 1. Category Margin & Discount Elasticity
- **Electronics** accounts for 38.6% of gross enterprise revenue (₹78.9L), but observed margin compression from 44.1% to 37.8% during promotional quarters.
- **Hypothesis Confirmed:** Discount tiers exceeding 10% yielded only a 6% increase in unit volume while reducing gross profit per order by 18.4%.
- **Action:** Cap promotional discount rates at 8% on high-ticket electronics (Laptops, Smartwatches).

## 2. Regional Dominance & Expansion Strategy
- **Top Hubs:** Hyderabad, Bangalore, and Mumbai generate over 52% of total retail sales.
- **Fastest Growing:** Chennai and Pune exhibited highest QoQ order count acceleration (+14.2%).
- **Action:** Pilot localized inventory fulfillment centers in Hyderabad and Bangalore to cut transit times from 4 days to next-day delivery.

## 3. Customer Retention & Churn Mitigation
- **Repeat Rate:** 78.3% of customers made 2 or more purchases.
- **RFM Segmentation:** 43 Champions, 70 Loyalists, and 7 high-value accounts identified at acute churn risk (>90 days without purchase).
- **Action:** Automated trigger campaigns offering 5% targeted loyalty vouchers to customers in the 'At Risk' segment.
